## Закадровый текст (целиком)

**S1 · 0:00–0:20**
September, seventeen ninety-five. [p] A valley south of Tbilisi. [P]
On one side, one of the greatest armies the Caucasus had seen. [p]
On the other, a small kingdom… [p] and three hundred men from the mountains. [P]
This is their story. [P]

**S2 · 0:20–0:50**
The kingdom was Kartli-Kakheti. [p] Its king, Erekle the Second, was seventy-five years old. [p] He had fought for half a century. [P]
Now Agha Mohammad Khan, ruler of the Qajar state, demanded that Georgia return to Persian rule. [p] Erekle refused. [P]
By treaty, Russia had promised to protect Georgia. [p] Its help never came. [P]

**S3 · 0:50–1:15**
Historians estimate the Qajar army at thirty to forty thousand. [p]
Erekle gathered around five thousand, [p] among them men from Imereti under King Solomon the Second. [P]
And from the valley of the Aragvi river, [p] the highlanders came down to defend the capital. [P]

**S4 · 1:15–1:50**
From the eighth of September, the Georgians held the approaches to Tbilisi. [P]
Wave after wave broke against them. [p] And still they held. [p]
Steel on steel. [p] Shield against shield. [P]
On the eleventh, the main assault began. [p] Smoke covered the field. [P]

**S5 · 1:50–2:10**
Outnumbered, abandoned by some of his nobles, [p] the Georgian line began to break. [P]
By tradition, the old king would not leave the field. [p]
If he fell, the kingdom fell with him. [P]

**S6 · 2:10–2:40**
So the Aragvians turned to face the storm. [P]
By tradition, they had sworn to fight to the death. [p]
They held the road, [p] while the king's last guard led him away to the mountains. [P]
They fell, [p] one by one. [P]
It is said that some fought their way almost to the khan's own banner. [P]
They kept their oath. [P]

**S7 · 2:40–3:00**
Tbilisi burned. [p] Some fifteen thousand people were carried into captivity. [P]
But Georgia endured. [P]
In two thousand eight, the Georgian Church named the three hundred Aragvians holy martyrs, [p] remembered on the eleventh of September, in the Church calendar. [p]
A monument stands where their graves were found. [p] A metro station bears their name. [P]
Three hundred. [p] Remember them.

## Раскадровка

Грузинское войско: красно-белые знамёна с крестом (без воспроизведения конкретных исторических знамён). Армия Каджаров: зелёно-золотые знамёна, без символов, которые можно счесть оскорбительными; показана как сильный, дисциплинированный противник, без карикатуры. ## Заметки
- Ранение царя не подтверждено (см. sources.md, п. 13) → в закадре его нет, сцена S6b «царя уводят» плюс пометка о драматизации.
- «Знамя шаха» заменено на «khan's banner»: в 1795 г. Ага-Мохаммед ещё не был коронован (коронация в 1796 г.); подано как предание.
- Все числа в кадре совпадают с sources.md: 30–40 000 / ≈5 000 / ≈15 000 пленных / 27.06.2008 / 11 сент. (24 сент.) / 1959 / 1967.
