## Story

### What is TAMADA?
In Georgia, the *tamada* is the person who leads the toasts. Their words make the whole table laugh, cry and raise a glass.

**TAMADA** turns that tradition into a fast, silly card game for friends. No phones, no long rules, just your imagination and a 30-second timer.

One player is the Tamada and opens a topic: *"A toast to… our teachers!"*
Everyone else picks one of their word cards and has **30 seconds** to give a toast that connects **both words on the card**:

> **Mother-in-law + Hypotenuse**
> **The last khinkali on the plate + World diplomacy**
> **"I'll be there in 5 minutes" + A Swiss watch**
> **The parents' group chat + A secret mission**

The Tamada picks the funniest toast, and the winner takes the topic card. Then the next player becomes the Tamada.

### How to play
1. **Draw a topic.** The Tamada reads it out loud.
2. **Link both words in 30 seconds.** Choose 1 of your 3 word cards and work both of its words into your toast.
3. **The Tamada picks the funniest.** The winner keeps the topic card.

With 3–4 players, the first to 3 topic cards wins. With 5–6 players, the first to 2 wins. A game takes 15–20 minutes, so there is always time for "one more round".

**Alaverdi cards.** Love someone's toast? Play an *Alaverdi* card and continue it with your own twist. If the Tamada likes your version better, you steal the point.

### What's in the box
- 24 topic cards
- 60 word cards with **2 words each** (120 words in total)
- 6 Alaverdi cards
- Rules printed on the back of the box
- 3–6 players · 15–20 minutes · ages 12+

Every word card holds two words that should never meet: one from home, school or Georgian life, one from science, tech or the big wide world. With 24 topics and 60 pairs there are over 1,400 combinations, so no two games are the same.

### Who it's for
TAMADA is made for **teenagers 12–16 and their friends**: after school, at a sleepover, on a birthday. It's a great gift from parents who'd love to see their kids laughing together instead of scrolling.

The game is **family-friendly**: no alcohol, no dating, no adult jokes. You raise a glass of juice or lemonade, not wine.

### Language
The first edition is **in Georgian**. It is made in Georgia, for Georgian kids. If the campaign goes well, an **English edition** is our first stretch goal (see below).

## Risks and challenges
This is our first board game, and we'd rather be honest than promise too much.

- **Printing.** We print with a local printer in Georgia and approve a physical sample before the full run. Delays at the printer are the most likely risk, and we will post an update every two weeks.
- **Language.** The Georgian text is checked by native-speaker proofreaders before printing.
- **Shipping.** Orders are packed and shipped by our team in Georgia. If anything arrives damaged, we replace it.

If something goes wrong, you'll hear it from us first.

## FAQ

**Can I play if I don't speak Georgian?**
Not this edition, because the cards are in Georgian. An English edition is our stretch goal.

**Is it okay for kids? It's about toasts.**
Yes. Toasts are about saying kind and funny things about people. The game has no alcohol, no dating and no adult jokes. Juice works perfectly.

**What if my kid freezes in 30 seconds?**
Every player holds 3 cards and picks the easiest pair. Our playtests showed [fill in after testing]. You can also play with a 45-second timer.

**Won't it get boring after a few games?**
There are 24 topics × 60 word pairs, more than 1,400 combinations, and the cards are reshuffled every game.
